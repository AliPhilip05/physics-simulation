# Session — November 6, 2026 (Weeks 9–10)

## Week 9: orientation-independent shape measures

Added principal RMS extents from eigenvalues of the centered position
covariance matrix. The minor-to-major ratio now uses those extents instead of
fixed x/y/z spreads, so rigidly rotating a given cloud does not change the
reported shape. The covariance describes positional spread; it is not the
mechanical inertia tensor.

## Week 10: controlled rotation

Added a solid-body rotating initial condition with angular speed
`omega = 0.5 / t0` around +z. Initial velocities are `v = omega cross r`; the
particle count, total mass, softening, outer radius, and pair force are
unchanged. Press `V` in 3D mode to toggle the spin and `A` to toggle spherical
versus oblate shape. `R` resets the selected combination. The display reports
the angular-momentum magnitude and principal shape extents.

This remains a cold collisionless particle model with nonzero organized
rotation, no pressure, and Newtonian softened gravity. The rotation is not a
fluid equilibrium. The covariance shape measures also do not imply a fluid
surface.
