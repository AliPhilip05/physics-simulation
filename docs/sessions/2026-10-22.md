## Concepts reviewed

We worked through assignment versus accumulation, frame duration versus frame
count, `f32` to `f64` conversion, nested defaults, associated functions versus
methods, trait implementations, field visibility, and text formatting.

The final comprehension check established that `step(0.1)` directly adds
`0.1` even when `paused` is true. The normal loop prevents that call while
paused. Earlier confusion about `=` versus `+=` was resolved: assignment
replaces the total, while addition assignment preserves and extends it.
