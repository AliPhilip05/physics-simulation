## Physics and Rust topics reviewed

We derived the outer material boundary's Newtonian equations from radial
velocity, Newton's second law, spherical symmetry, and the shell theorem.
The model assumes pressureless radial motion with fixed enclosed mass.

Normalized scales are `r = R / R0`, `tau = t / t0`, and `v = V / v0`, with
`t0 = sqrt(R0^3 / (G M))` and `v0 = R0 / t0`. Gravity is `a = -1 / r^2`.
No physical duration can be assigned before choosing `R0` and `M`.

We worked through velocity Verlet's starting acceleration, proposed position,
ending acceleration, and velocity update. We also discussed shared and mutable
receivers, owned candidate states, constructors versus methods, `Option`,
return expressions, and validation of positive finite timesteps and valid state.
Negative radial velocity represents inward motion and is valid.
